BarPath is a barbell path tracking app for strength training.

Test Account: Not required (no login)

Demo Instructions:
1. Grant camera permission when prompted
2. Tap "Record" and perform a barbell lift (or simulate with hand movements)
3. Tap "Stop" to analyze
4. View results with bar path overlay and metrics

The app uses Vision framework for pose detection. All processing is on-device.
