BarPath is the ultimate barbell path tracking app for serious lifters. Using advanced computer vision, BarPath analyzes your lifts in real-time and provides actionable coaching feedback.

FEATURES:
• Real-time bar path tracking during recording
• Bilateral wrist detection with Kalman filtering for precision
• Intelligent coaching tips based on your technique
• Side-by-side comparison of different analyses
• 1RM calculator using velocity-based training
• Export annotated videos to Photos
• Rep-by-rep breakdown with detailed metrics
• Dark mode support

PERFECT FOR:
• Powerlifters optimizing bar path
• Olympic weightlifters analyzing technique
• Strength coaches training athletes
• Anyone serious about improving their lifts

METRICS TRACKED:
• Peak and average velocity per rep
• Vertical displacement
• Horizontal drift
• Rep consistency
• Fatigue detection

PRIVACY FIRST:
All video processing happens on your device. No cloud uploads, no data collection, complete privacy.

• iPhone running iOS 26.0 or later
