BarPath Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 7, 2026

Privacy First: BarPath is designed with your privacy as a top priority. All video processing happens on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data.

Information Collection and Use

BarPath does NOT collect any personal information. We do not:

  • Collect your videos or images
  • Track your usage or behavior
  • Share any data with third parties
  • Use analytics or tracking services
  • Store data on external servers

Camera Access

BarPath requires camera access to record your lifting sessions and track barbell path in real-time using computer vision. All video processing occurs entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Videos are only saved locally when you choose to export them.

Photo Library Access

BarPath requests photo library access solely to save analyzed videos with bar path overlays to your Photos app. This only occurs when you explicitly choose to save or export a video. We do not access or read any existing photos in your library.

On-Device Processing

All barbell tracking, pose detection, analysis, and coaching feedback happens entirely on your iPhone using:

  • Apple Vision framework for pose detection
  • Local processing for bar path analysis
  • On-device storage for your analysis history

No internet connection is required to use BarPath's core features.

Data Storage

BarPath stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Analysis Results: Bar path metrics, rep data, and coaching tips
  • App Settings: Your preferences (lift type, haptic feedback, etc.)
  • Purchase Status: Whether you've purchased the lifetime unlock

This data remains on your device and is never uploaded to any server.

In-App Purchases

BarPath offers a one-time lifetime purchase managed through Apple's StoreKit. Purchase information is handled securely by Apple and follows their privacy policies. We only verify purchase status locally on your device.

Third-Party Services

BarPath does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The only external service used is Apple's StoreKit for in-app purchases, which is managed entirely by Apple.

Children's Privacy

BarPath is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
